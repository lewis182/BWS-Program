Prepare

  1. Brisk walk or march: 3–5 minutes
  2. Arm circles: 5 each direction
  3. Bodyweight good mornings: 8 reps
  4. Squat-to-stand: 5 controlled reps
  5. Very light side-lying dumbbell external rotation: 8 each side
  6. Dead bug: 5 each side
Ramp-up sets
Before the first leg and upper-body lift, perform 2–3 lighter practice sets. Start near 50% of the working weight, then 70%, then one easy rep near 85%. Warm-ups should prepare you, not tire you.
